Approach

How I work

I usually start with the business rule, check whether the data matches it, find the exceptions, and then figure out what process or control would prevent the issue from happening again.

01

Analyze

Use SQL, Excel, and BI reporting to check the data, quantify the problem, and identify which issues matter most.

02

Govern

Turn findings into CRM rules, routing logic, stage requirements, approvals, and repeatable processes.

03

Communicate

Summarize the work in dashboards, process maps, SOPs, and recommendations that other teams can actually use.
